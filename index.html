Active Ragdoll 2D — Stick Figure

No animation clips. No transform hacks. Just physics.

Bring the wobbly, cartoony charm of active-ragdoll games to your 2D project. The stick figure is a real ragdoll at all times — eleven rigidbodies driven by muscle-like joint motors, steered through the pelvis — so control stays crisp no matter how floppy you dial the body.

Features

Everything a stick fight needs

Physics-only locomotion

Procedural walk cycle, jumps and air control. Two balance modes: an easy hover hip, or true self-balancing that can fall and get back up.

Floppiness dial

From stiff and controlled to rubber puppet with a single slider — stiffness, strength and damping of all muscles at once.

Impact reactions & knockout

Only the struck region gives way. Hit hard enough and every muscle cuts out — a double-tap on jump wakes the figure again.

Items & inventory

Pick up with either hand, throw with spin, carry a ring of weapons on the back, wear headgear. Custom items are one component away.

Physical melee

Real arc swings and tip-first thrusts, a two-handed greatsword and an axe, bare-fisted punches — plus blocking with shields and blades.

Ranged weapons

Bow and arrows, a full-auto rifle whose recoil really throws the aim off, and a rocket launcher with knockout blasts.

Grab & climb

Grab objects, walls or other figures. Alternate left and right grips to climb ropes, walls.

Health & hit feedback

Modular health system with body-part multipliers, health bars, hit sparks, camera shake, hit flash and splatter in the figure's own colour.

9 code-built environments

Day, sunset, night, forest, city, desert, winter, castle and sci-fi — with parallax, props, lighting and a comic outline. Zero image assets.

Enemy AI template

A pursuing enemy stickman shows how to drive a figure from code — the starting point for your own brains.

One-click generator

GameObject → 2D Active Ragdoll → Stick Figure. Pick size, colour, muscle strength and balance mode, press Create, press Play.

A word on how it was made

Built with AI, tuned by hand

Large parts of the code and documentation were written with AI assistance — guided by years of being an active-ragdoll fan. Every tuning decision, from muscle feel to knockout thresholds, reflects that hands-on experience.

And because the result is plain, well-documented C# with zero binary assets, AI coding assistants can read and extend the entire package: point yours at the included guides and let it build new weapons, headgear or enemy brains for you.
